GPX Route Weather
Upload one or more GPX routes. The app will build the route, elevation profile, ride timing and along-the-way weather forecast from the files.

Long route detected

Later GPX stages are dated automatically on consecutive days.
Dates are taken from GPX timestamps when available. Otherwise they default from tomorrow onward and can be changed here.
GPX parsing happens in your browser. When the forecast is built, route sample coordinates are sent to the weather service and map tiles are requested in the same way as the GTVI weather app.
