About this forecast

This app is designed for the Vienna–Pula ride and combines several weather forecasts with the planned route and timing.

  • Forecast consensus shows how closely the main forecasts agree. It is a guide to confidence, not an official probability.
  • Forecast spread shows the broader spread of likely conditions, using ensemble forecasting to highlight uncertainty.
  • Weather along the way is matched to your expected position on the route, based on your start time, riding speed and planned stops.
  • Changing the Ride plan changes the point-by-point weather timing, but does not change the overall daily forecast consensus.
  • Forecasts become more reliable as the ride day approaches, so refresh the app regularly and check again before setting off.

The route is embedded in the app; live weather data and map tiles require an internet connection.
